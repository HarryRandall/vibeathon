## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are ANU students navigating a Canvas-style study workspace for day-to-day course work. They need to scan deadlines quickly, jump between course areas without friction, and understand what matters this week without hunting through dense LMS pages.

### Brand Personality
Confident, academic, contemporary. The interface should feel trustworthy and structured like an institutional tool, but more polished and legible than stock Canvas. It should reduce stress, not amplify it.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light theme first. Keep the familiar Canvas shell and ANU gold/black reference points from the downloaded mocks, then modernise the page interiors with cleaner hierarchy, softer surfaces, stronger spacing rhythm, and compact information panels. Avoid trendy dark gradients, generic startup dashboards, and decorative clutter that fights the academic content.

### Design Principles
1. Preserve recognisable LMS navigation while improving readability inside the content area.
2. Prefer reusable, typed content structures over one-off JSX fragments.
3. Surface the most useful course actions and deadlines first.
4. Use restrained polish: warm neutrals, subtle depth, and clear typographic hierarchy.
5. Make layouts adapt cleanly from full-width desktop to narrower course views without losing context.
